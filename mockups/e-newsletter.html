Scalable Phased Arrays with QuadRF

A low-cost, open-source phased array SDR brings beamforming and direction finding within reach of radio amateurs.

Presented by Roy Gross KM6EOOFriday, 6 November 2026 · 7:00 PM PacificCubberley Community Center and Zoom

Phased arrays are a core technology for RF professionals: direction finding, beamforming, interferometry, imaging, and MIMO are essentials of modern wireless engineering. Until now, these capabilities have been prohibitively expensive for radio amateurs.

Roy will break down the QuadRF's Raspberry Pi-based architecture, discuss clever DSP techniques, and show it live as a real-time AR radio camera, with a look at mesh networks, data links, sensing, and even a 240-element array for moonbounce.

He attended his first PAARA meeting in fifth grade, and got his ticket soon after.About the presenter

Roy is an undergrad studying electrical engineering at UC Santa Barbara, and building phased arrays with ScaleRF. He enjoys homebrewing antennas and radios, and biking and hiking to operate HF portable.
