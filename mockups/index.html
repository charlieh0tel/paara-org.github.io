PAARA design mockups Draft

Thirteen directions for paara.org, now with inside pages. Each mockup has a light/dark toggle and works at phone width.

Most of the site is long pages: meetings, Field Day, repeaters, bylaws. Six options are whole-site styles, shown here with the November meeting page. The other seven are themed front pages; each would use a plainer whole-site style for its inside pages.
