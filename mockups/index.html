PAARA design mockups Draft

Narrowing the choice for paara.org to two decisions. Each mockup has a light/dark toggle and works at phone width.

1. Inside pages

Most of the site is long pages: meetings, Field Day, repeaters, bylaws. This style covers all of them, so it is the main decision. Each choice is shown as a home page and the November meeting page.

  1. A: Clean clubWhite, red accent, seven-item top menu. Home pageMeeting page
  2. M: RefreshToday's layout and sidebar links, with better type and spacing. The least change. Home pageMeeting page

2. Front page

The home page can match the inside pages, or be a themed front door that leads into them. A themed front page is a second design to maintain, and has to look like it belongs with the inside pages.

  1. Match the inside pagesA's or M's own home page, above.
  2. D: QSL cardQSL cards, log sheets and rubber stamps.
  3. F: SignalWaterfall display, at-a-glance info, repeater table.
  4. H: Swiss posterStrict grid, huge type, PAARA in Morse.
  5. I: 1937 radio dialArt Deco cabinet with a dial tuned to the net.
  6. J: Bay Area mapWhere we meet, operate Field Day, and run repeaters.
  7. K: AgendaCalendar-first: what's on this month.
  8. L: Packet BBSTerminal screen with menu commands; amber at night.

On these mockups, the themed front pages' Meetings links open the A meeting page, to show how a themed front leads into plain inside pages.
