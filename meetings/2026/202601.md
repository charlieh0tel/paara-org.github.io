## Details

Chris Maughan, G7LQX studied Electronics at university, but quickly moved over to software and has worked at NVIDIA for 26 years, developing software debugging and analysis tools for game developers.  These days, now his children have gone off into the world, he uses the extra space and time to indulge his inner maker, focusing on electronics, radio and computer projects.
